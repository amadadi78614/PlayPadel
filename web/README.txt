Padel Doubles 3D - installable web app (PWA)

Put the contents of this folder on any HTTPS web host. Players open the link on their phone,
tap "Install app" (Android) or Share > "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) and it runs like an app,
full screen, offline after the first visit. Rankings and feedback need internet.

Quickest:  https://app.netlify.com/drop  -> drag this folder onto the page -> you get a link.
Your own domain: point a sub-domain such as play.lowveldpadel.co.za at that host.
