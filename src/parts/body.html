  • Simple (default): left thumb runs your player (a stick appears where you touch); right thumb swipes to hit. Swipe up towards where you want the ball to go; a hard flick hits a power drive. Short, soft swipe = drop shot (chiquita from the back). Swipe down = lob. Tap = safe shot. On a high ball: straight swipe = bandeja, hard straight flick = smash, angled swipe (about 45°, left or right) = víbora. Swipe as the ring closes for a Perfect hit. When the SUPER bar is full, a hard flick fires a super shot. Serve: move with the left thumb, tap the right side to bounce the ball, then swipe up (angle it to aim at the T or wide).
    • 
      
  • Console: your left thumb runs your player and aims (push left or right as you hit to send the ball that way). On the right, hold DRIVE, LOB, DROP or SMASH to build power and release as the ring closes on you. DRIVE on a high ball plays a bandeja (víbora if you aim wide); SMASH on a low ball is a power drive. To serve, steer the target with your left thumb and hold and release any shot button.
    • 
      
  • Stick-style, one thumb anywhere: flick towards where you want the ball to go; a long, fast flick hits harder and smashes a high ball; a short, soft flick plays a drop shot (at the net) or chiquita (from the back); flick down for a lob; a tap plays a safe drive (bandeja on a high ball); flick wide on a high ball for a víbora. Your player runs automatically, but press, hold and drag to move yourself whenever you want. To serve, drag the target and release. Classic gives you the joystick and separate buttons.
    • 
      
  • Timing: as the ball comes, a ring shrinks onto your orange marker. Swing when it turns yellow for a Perfect hit: more pace, pinpoint aim and a moment of slow motion. Early pulls the ball across, late pushes it wide.
    • 
      
  • Aim assist sends un-aimed shots to the open side, gives a little extra reach and steps you towards the ball. Turn it off for full control.
    • 
      
  • Graphics: Auto lowers detail if your phone struggles.
